# Relógio

The second header tab, `#relogio` (`#relogio/YYYY-MM-DD` for another date), shows the Apple Watch values. A date stepper (region `Data do relógio`) picks the date, never past today. The region `Relógio` is a table of every Health Sample metric with three columns: `Dia`, `Semana` (Monday to Sunday) and `Mês`, each the average over the days that have a value, with `<n> dias` under a week or month value, and `—` where no day has one. Only days up to today count. Under it, `Ligar Apple Watch` opens the panel that brings the values in: the signed Shortcut, its address, a manual paste, and the workouts of a Health export.

## Sub-features

- `relogio-card` is the region `Relógio`: rows `Horas em pé (h)`, `Tempo em pé (min)`, `Passos`, `Energia ativa (kcal)`, `Exercício (min)`, `Sono` (as `7 h 32 min`) and `FC em repouso (bpm)`, and the columns `Dia <d mês>`, `Semana <d–d mês>` and `Mês <mês>`.
- `relogio-date` moves the card with `Dia anterior` and `Dia seguinte` (disabled on today), and `Voltar a hoje` on a past date. Each step writes `#relogio/<date>`.
- `relogio-health-shortcut` is a link `Instalar atalho` whose href is `<origin>/api/health/shortcut?url=<origin>/api/health` (url-encoded). Opening the panel fetches it once: on a Mac the server signs the Shortcut with `shortcuts sign` (about 3 s, then cached per address) and answers a download `Treino - Saude.shortcut` whose bytes start `AEA1`; where it cannot sign it answers 503 and the panel shows the message (role `alert`) instead of the link. The Shortcut itself (`server/healthShortcut.ts`) cannot be run here: it needs an iPhone with Health. `e2e/watch.mjs` checks the href and, on a Mac, the download.
- `relogio-health-import` opens a panel named `Ligar Apple Watch`. A hint says the Shortcut sends the last 14 days and today each time it runs. `.health-latest` reads `Último valor do relógio: <d> <mês>` (the latest date with a value) or `Ainda não chegou nenhum valor do relógio.`. A read-only textbox `Endereço do Atalho` holds `<origin>/api/health`, and `Copiar` copies it and says (role `status`) `Endereço copiado.`. On a `localhost` or `127.0.0.1` origin a hint next to `Instalar atalho` says `Aberta em localhost, a app dá um atalho que só funciona neste computador. …`. Four numbered steps cover installing the Shortcut, allowing Health and an optional automation, and a line says the Shortcut does not send sleep or stand hours. A collapsed `<details>` `Colar manualmente` holds a `Dados do Apple Watch` textarea and `Colar da área de transferência`, which fills it from the clipboard or, when the browser refuses, says (role `status`) `O navegador não deixou ler a área de transferência. Cola o texto na caixa.` Valid JSON shows `.backup-summary` `<n> valores, de <d/m> a <d/m>` (`1 valor`, and no `a <d/m>` when every value is on one date, as in `2 valores, de 7/10`) and enables `Importar`; an invalid paste shows a role `alert` naming the first bad row, such as `Valor n.º 2: a métrica "sittingHours" não existe. Não foi alterado nada.`, and `Importar` stays disabled. The format is in the README's Apple Watch import section.
- `relogio-watch-workouts` is the region `Treinos do relógio` in that panel, before `Colar manualmente`. A hint gives the latest imported date (`Último treino importado: <d> <mês>.` or `Ainda não importaste treinos do relógio.`) and says the file stays on the device; four steps say how to export from Saúde. `Escolher ficheiro` opens the hidden input `Exportação do Saúde` (accepts `.zip` and `.xml`). While it reads, a role `status` says `A ler o ficheiro… <n> %`. Then `.backup-summary` reads `<n> treinos do relógio, de <d/m> a <d/m>` (or `O ficheiro não tem treinos.`, with the button disabled), a hint under it says `<n> novos.` when some are already in the app, `Já estão todos na app.` when all are, and `<n> treinos não se leram e ficam de fora.` for skipped Workout elements, and `Importar treinos` imports, closes the panel and says `Importados <summary>.`. A zip without an export.xml or a broken one shows a role `alert`. Importing the same file again changes no stored byte.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- The `Relógio` link in the header `nav` named `Secções`, or `#relogio[/<date>]`.
- `Ligar Apple Watch`, under the card.

## Driving it with session.mjs

Preconditions: `openApp` opens `#treino`, so call `app.view('Relógio')` first.

- **Card.** `watchDay(page)` from `e2e/_driver.mjs` reads the `Dia` column as `<row> <value>` joined by ` · `, leaving out `—`. A fresh server reads `''`.
- **Date.** Click `Dia anterior` in `region('Data do relógio')`, then `page.waitForURL('**#relogio/<yesterday>')`.
- **Apple Watch, posted.** `page.request.post(new URL('/api/health', APP_URL).href, { headers: { 'content-type': 'application/json' }, data: json })` answers `{ count, from, to }`. Then `page.evaluate(() => document.dispatchEvent(new Event('visibilitychange')))`: the open tab shows the values without a reload, and sends nothing back (the server's health rev is unchanged). `e2e/watch.mjs` drives this.
- **Watch workouts.** Build a zip in the script with `buildZip` from `src/zipFixture.ts` (rows `{ name: 'apple_health_export/export.xml', data: xml }`), click `Ligar Apple Watch`, then `region('Treinos do relógio').getByLabel('Exportação do Saúde').setInputFiles({ name: 'export.zip', mimeType: 'application/zip', buffer })`. Wait for `.backup-summary`, click `Importar treinos`, then `synced(page)`: `app.stored()['watch-workouts']` is `{ version: 1, workouts: [...] }`. `e2e/watch-workouts.mjs` is the full recipe.
- **Apple Watch, pasted.** Grant `clipboard-read` and `clipboard-write` on the context, `navigator.clipboard.writeText(json)` from the page, click `Ligar Apple Watch`, then the text `Colar manualmente`, then `Colar da área de transferência`, then `Importar` (exact). The panel closes and `.backup-done` (role `status`) reads `Importados 2 valores, de <d/m>.` (` a <d/m>` for a range). Close the panel with `Fechar` (exact). On the Relógio tab, `watchDay(page)` reads `Horas em pé (h) 9 · Passos 8 123 · Sono 7 h 32 min` for a paste of today's `standHours` 9, `steps` 8123 and `sleepMinutes` 452. Repeat the import: `app.stored().health` is byte-identical.

## Gotchas

- A Health export is real health data. Never pick the user's own export.zip in a scenario; build one in the script.
- A merge never overwrites a watch value, while a post to `/api/health` or a paste always does: the backup is old data, the Shortcut's is new.
- `/api/health` has no reset and no login. It writes the test server's temp database like any other write; never point it at port 5180.
- Watch Workouts show on `Treino` → `Registo` too, as the list `Treinos do relógio` under the date stepper (see [workouts](./workouts.md)).
