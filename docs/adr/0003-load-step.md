# The load moves one step per session: 1 kg under 25 kg, 2,5 kg from 25 kg

ACSM puts a load increase at 2–10% (`docs/research/progression-rate.md`). A fixed 2,5 kg step is a 25% jump on a 10 kg dumbbell, so loads under 25 kg move by 1 kg. The load never moves more than one step in a session, however easy the sets felt; no evidence ties progression speed to injury, so this cap is the only guard. Steps do not depend on the user's actual equipment (Material).
