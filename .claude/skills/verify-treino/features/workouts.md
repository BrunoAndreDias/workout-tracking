# Workouts (Treino)

The `Treino` view is the user's workout log. Under the header it has three tabs in a `nav` named `Treino`: `Registo` logs the sessions of one date, and `Plano de treino` holds the user's Workout Plans, and `Material` holds the Locais and each Exercise's material (see [equipment](./equipment.md)). A plan is a Training Split, 3 days (`Push`, `Pull`, `Full body`) or 4 (`Upper A`, `Lower A`, `Upper B`, `Lower B`), made of Training Blocks that each have those days. A new plan comes filled from the split's template with English catalog exercises (ported from just-workout); the user swaps, adds and removes them. Starting a session fills each set with a suggested target (double progression from last time), and one tap on ✓ marks a set done as shown.

## Sub-features

- `workout-open` opens the view through the `Treino` link or `#treino`, on today. `#treino/YYYY-MM-DD` opens that date.
- `workout-plan-create` is the card `Novo plano de treino`: shown when there is no plan, or after `+ Novo plano` in the `nav` named `Planos de treino` (with `Cancelar`). It has the textbox `Nome` (default `Plano de treino <n>`), the group `Divisão` with two `.split-tile` buttons (`3 dias` `Push · Pull · Full body`, `4 dias` `Upper A · Lower A · Upper B · Lower B`; the chosen one `aria-pressed="true"`, 3 days by default) and `Criar plano`. The new plan has one `Bloco A` of 6 weeks with RIR targets `4–5`, `3`, `2–3`, `2`, `1–2`, `0–1` and every day filled. The selected plan tab is the active plan; `Eliminar` deletes it after a confirm, and logged sessions stay.
- `workout-plan-rename` renames a plan through `Nome do plano de treino: <name>. Clique para renomear`. Days cannot be renamed, added or removed. A muted line under the title names the split's days.
- `workout-blocks` is the region `Blocos` under the plan's title: one `.block-card` button per Training Block in order, with `.block-card-name` and `.block-card-meta` `<n> semanas` (`1 semana`). The selected card has `aria-pressed="true"` (the current block until another is clicked); the current block's card has `aria-current="true"`, a `.track` progress bar and `Semana <w> de <n>`. `+ Novo bloco` copies the selected block (weeks, RIR targets, days and their exercises) as the next free letter, `Bloco B`, and selects it. Muted lines read `Só contam as semanas com treino. Uma semana sem treino não avança o bloco.` and, until the cycle starts, `Ainda não começaste. Usa "Começar aqui" no primeiro bloco.`
- `workout-block-edit` is the card of the selected block: `Nome do bloco: <name>. Clique para renomear`, `Semanas de <block>` (1 to 52), one `RIR da semana <w> de <block>` field per week (`3`, `5-6`, `5–6` or `5 a 6`, 0 to 10; shows `5–6` once it loses focus; empty is no target, placeholder `—`), `Começar aqui` (starts the cycle on this block this week; a confirm when it was already started) and `Remover bloco` (a confirm; absent with one block). Fewer weeks drop the last RIR targets.
- `workout-day` is the `nav` named `Dias de <block>`: one `.tab` per day of the split, the shown one `aria-current="page"`. Under it the day's card is a region named after the day.
- `workout-planned` is each row of the day: `.role-label` (`Principal`, `Secundário`, `Isolamento` or `Abs`, over the select `Função de <exercise>`), `.planned-name`, `Séries de <exercise>` (a whole number) × `Repetições de <exercise>` (`8`, `8-12`, `8–12` or `8 a 12`, shown `8–12` once it loses focus), `Semanas de <exercise>` (the Exercise Run, 1 to 52; empty is no limit, placeholder `∞`) and the unit `semanas`, `Trocar <exercise>` (⇄) and `Remover <exercise> de <day>`. A role sets the rep range and the run of a template or added row: Principal 6–8 with no limit, Secundário 8–10, Isolamento and Abs 10–15, each 8 weeks, 3 sets; changing the role later keeps the sets, reps and weeks. On the current block (the first before the cycle starts) each row's `.planned-main` ends with `.run-line` `semana <w> de <n>` (`semana <w> · sem limite`), or `.missing-line` `Rever: <n> semanas feitas` once the run is done. In a block after the first, an Exercise the block before has in none of its days reads `<exercise> · novo` (`.planned-name.planned-new`).
- `workout-muscles` shows the muscle groups. Beside the day's planned list (the card is `.workout-day.day-trains`, the list in `.day-planned`), the aside `Músculos de <day>` (`.day-muscle-sets`, heading `.day-muscles-title` `Músculos neste dia`) has one `.day-muscle` per trained group in the picker's filter order: `.muscle-name`, `.muscle-sets` `<n> séries` and a `.track.muscle-track` bar as wide as its sets over the day's most (Push: Peito 3, Ombros 6, Tríceps 3, Abdominais 3; Full body: Peito 3, Costas 6, Ombros 3, Quadríceps 3, Posteriores 3, Bíceps 3, Tríceps 3, Glúteos 3, Gémeos 3). The user's own Exercises are left out of it, and it is absent, the list full width, when no catalog Exercise is in the day. At ≤640px it sits above the list as a wrapped line with no bars. Below the day's card, the region `Músculos por semana` (`.block-muscles`, heading `.blocks-title`) has one `.muscle-row` per group, all 11: `.muscle-name`, a `.track.muscle-track` bar as wide as its sets over the block's most, `.muscle-sets` `<n> séries` (`1 série`) and `.muscle-days` the days that train it (`Pull · Full body`). An untrained group is `.muscle-row.muscle-gap` with `—` and no days. A set counts in full for each primary muscle of its catalog Exercise, each day once a week. The user's own Exercises read `Sem grupo muscular: Supino (4 séries)`; a muted line closes with `Séries planeadas por semana, contando cada dia uma vez.` It follows `Séries de <exercise>`, swaps, adds and removals at once. The 3-day template gives Peito 6, Costas 12, Ombros 12, Quadríceps 3, Posteriores 3, Bíceps 6, Tríceps 6, Antebraços —, Abdominais 6, Glúteos 3, Gémeos 3.
- `workout-picker` and `workout-muscle-map` are the exercise picker (`Adicionar exercício`, `Trocar exercício`), each catalog row with a `Ver músculos de <exercise>` figure button that shows where the Exercise is felt (`Alvo`, `Apoio`) on a body map: see [picker](./picker.md).
- `workout-date` is the `.date-stepper` region named `Data do treino`, which moves the logged date with `Dia anterior` and `Dia seguinte`. `Dia seguinte` is disabled on today, and `Voltar a hoje` shows on any other date. The date is in the URL hash.
- `workout-watch` is a list named `Treinos do relógio` under the date stepper (and under `Treinos anteriores` when that shows), one `.workout-line` per Watch Workout on the date, by start: `RelógioForça tradicional18:02 · 52 min · 311 kcal`, with ` · <km> km` when the watch has a distance. It is read only and absent on a date with none. The workouts come from the Health export picked in `Ligar Apple Watch` (see [backup](./backup.md)).
- `workout-start` offers, on a date with no session, `Começar <day>` (primary): the day after the latest session's day on or before the date, counted by its place in the block, so the rotation carries into a new block; the first day when no session started from this plan. Then the other days of the current block (its first block before the cycle starts) and `Treino livre`. Above them, `.block-line` reads `<block> · semana <w> de <n>`, plus ` · RIR <target>` when that week has one, or just `<block>` before the cycle starts.
- `workout-block-notice` is the region `Novo bloco` at the top of that card on week 1 of a block the cycle moved into (not on the week the cycle was started): `Começa o <block> esta semana.`, then `Entram: <exercises>` and `Saem: <exercises>` against the block before, each line absent when empty. Weeks count only when they hold a session with a done set, so a week without training pauses the block. A future date shows `Dia futuro. Só podes registar até hoje.` The card's title is `Sem treino registado`, `Registar as séries` when the date has a Watch Workout, or `Outro treino` after `+ Outro treino neste dia`. With no workout plan the card offers only `Treino livre`, under `Ainda não tens plano de treino. Cria um em Plano de treino, ou regista um treino livre.`
- `workout-runs` is the region `Exercícios a rever`, a `.block-notice` at the top of the start card on today or a past date, listing each Exercise of the active plan's current block whose run is done: `.run-row` `<exercise> · <n> semanas feitas` with `Trocar <exercise>` (text `Trocar`) and `Mais 4 semanas de <exercise>` (text `Mais 4 semanas`). `Trocar` opens `Trocar exercício` for it, and the pick swaps it on every day of the block it is in, its run starting today. `Mais 4 semanas` sets its weeks to the weeks done plus 4 (at most 52) on each of those days. The region is absent when no run is done. A run counts training weeks as a block does, from the Monday of the date the Exercise was added or swapped in, or its plan created; one from before runs counts from the week it was first done.
- `workout-suggest` gives one group per planned Exercise with the planned number of sets, each pending at the suggested target: from the done sets of the latest earlier session with any, at the last set's load, reps = (reps + RIR logged, or the RIR aimed at) − this week's RIR target, +1 when the target is unchanged and no set was harder than planned. Above the range the load goes up 2,5 kg, below it goes down; a bodyweight Exercise (no kg) moves reps only. The week's RIR target is its lowest number, never under 1 for Principal and Secundário, 2 when the week has none. `.lift-note` reads `Plano <sets> × <range>. ` and the reason, e.g. `Última: 62,5 kg × 7. Faz 8 com 62,5 kg.` An Exercise never done starts with empty sets and no reason.
- `workout-sets` is each `.set-row`: `Kg da série <n> de <exercise>` (comma decimals; empty is bodyweight, placeholder `—`) × `Repetições da série <n> de <exercise>`, `.set-rir` `RIR <n>` when set, the ✓ button `Série <n> de <exercise> feita` (`aria-pressed`; disabled with no reps; again un-marks) and `Remover série <n> de <exercise>`. Typing never marks a set done. The first Exercise with a set to do shows the group `RIR da série <n> de <exercise>` of `RIR <0–4 | 5 ou mais> na série <n> de <exercise>` chips below its sets, named for its first pending set; on an Exercise started with a target, the target's RIR chip has `.rir-target`, and a pick also fills the next pending set with no RIR. `Adicionar série a <exercise>` copies the last set, pending; `Remover <exercise>` removes the Exercise from the session. `.lift-count` reads `<done>/<sets>`.
- `workout-collapse` turns an Exercise whose sets are all done into one `.lift-summary` button (`<exercise>` and `.lift-check` `✓ <n> séries`); tapping reopens it, with `Fechar`. The session's foot reads `.sets-done` `<done>/<total> séries feitas`, then `.session-muscles` `Músculos: <group> <sets> · …`: the muscle groups the done sets trained, in the picker's order, each set counted for every primary muscle of its catalog Exercise (`Músculos: Ombros 3`). It follows each ✓ and is absent while no catalog Exercise has a done set.
- `workout-session-exercise` adds an Exercise to a session through `Exercício` and the picker. It starts with the sets it had the last time it was done, pending, or one empty set.
- `workout-exercise-note` is the `Nota` toggle beside `+ Série` in each Exercise's group (named `Nota de <exercise>`, `aria-expanded`). It opens a textarea of the same name, placeholder `Dores, desconforto, como correu`, with a `Ditar nota` button and `.note-times` once it holds text. Closed with text, the toggle shows `.notes-dot`. It opens by itself on load when the note has text.
- `workout-notes` uses `Notas do treino`, about the workout as a whole, with `.note-times` under it (`Escrito a 6 out, 18:50 · editado 19:02`).
- `workout-end-feel` is the group `No fim do treino` in a session's foot, before `Notas do treino`: `No fim do treino: <word>` buttons on the five words from `Muito mal` to `Muito bem`. The pressed one has `aria-pressed="true"`; tapping it again removes it. Stored as the session's `feel`, 1 to 5.
- `workout-after-list` is the group `Depois do treino` under it, present once the session has After-effects. Each `.after-item` reads when (`Dia seguinte`, `2 dias depois`, `3 dias depois`, or the date further on), what (`Treino` or the Exercise's name), the note text and its `.note-times`, by date, the whole session first.
- `workout-recent` is the region `Treinos anteriores`, above the date's session cards, on today or a past date. It lists each session one to three days before the date, newest first, as a group named `<title>, <ontem | há 2 dias | há 3 dias>`, with `.recent-meta` `ontem · no fim: Bem` when the session has an end feel. In each group the textbox `Como está o corpo` is the After-effect of the whole session for the date, and each Exercise has a `Nota de <exercise>` toggle (`aria-expanded`, `.notes-dot` when closed with text, open by itself when it has a note for the date) that opens a textbox of the same name. Blank text removes the After-effect. After-effects of the session from other dates show under `Noutros dias`, read only. The card is absent when no session falls in those three days, and on a future date.
- `workout-delete` uses `Eliminar treino`, after a confirm.
- `workout-another` uses `+ Outro treino neste dia` to start a second session on a date that has one.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- The `Treino` link in the header `nav` named `Secções`, or opening the app on `#treino`.

## Driving it with session.mjs

Preconditions:

- Fresh context, then `app.view('Treino')`. There is no workout plan and no session. Build state through the UI. `e2e/_driver.mjs` has `createWorkoutPlan(page, { name, days })`, `workoutDay(page, name)`, `addExercise(scope, name)`, `swapExercise(scope, from, to)` and `pickExercise(page, name)`.

- **Plan.** Click `Plano de treino` in the `nav` named `Treino`, then `createWorkoutPlan(page, { name: 'Força', days: 3 })`. The `Dias de Bloco A` tabs are `Push`, `Pull`, `Full body`; Push's `.planned-name`s are `Flat Barbell Bench Press`, `Standing Overhead Barbell Press`, `Dumbbell Lateral Raises`, `Cable Press-Downs`, `Cable Crunches`, `.role-label`s `Principal`, `Secundário`, `Isolamento`, `Isolamento`, `Abs`. Click `Trocar Flat Barbell Bench Press`: the dialog's region `Mesmo movimento` lists `Flat Dumbbell Bench Press` first. `addExercise(push, 'Supino')` creates it as `Isolamento`; searching `supino ` again shows it disabled under `Os teus`.
- **First session.** Click `Registo`, then `Dia anterior`, then `Começar Push`. A never-done Exercise has empty sets and its ✓ is disabled. Fill kg and reps, then ✓ each set: the Exercise collapses to `✓ <n> séries`, and `.sets-done` counts.
- **Persist.** `reloadApp(page)`: values, ticks and notes are back. `app.stored()['workout-sessions']` is version 3; each set is `{ kg, reps, rir, done }`, an Exercise started with history has `target: { kg, reps, rir }`. `app.stored()['workout-plans']` is version 3 with `split` `ppf` or `ul`.
- **Suggestion.** These numbers need the setup of `e2e/workout.mjs`: Supino set to `Principal`, `4` sets, `6-8` (the bodyweight example is the `Secundário` 8–10 `Standing Overhead Barbell Press`), with no `Começar aqui` (so the week's RIR target is 2). On the `Isolamento` 10–15 row `addExercise` makes, the same sets suggest 10 × `57,5` (`…por isso desce para 57,5 kg e faz 10.`). Another day, `Push`: Supino after 8 × 60 and 7 × `62,5` (no RIR, no target) suggests 8 × `62,5` on every set, pending, `.lift-note` ending `Última: 62,5 kg × 7. Faz 8 com 62,5 kg.`; a bodyweight 10, 9, 8 at RIR 2, 2 and none suggests 9 reps with no kg. `Começar` names the day after the last one: `Começar Pull`.
- **Blocks.** On a 4-day plan, `+ Novo bloco`, then in Bloco B's `Upper A` remove `Standing Barbell Curls` and add `Cable Curls` (`Cable Curls · novo`). Back on Bloco A, `Semanas de Bloco A` `1` and `Começar aqui`. To reach week 1 of Bloco B, `putStore` the plans with `start.since` two Mondays ago and a version 3 session with a done set on last Monday from Bloco A's `Upper A`, then reload: `Novo bloco` reads `Começa o Bloco B esta semana.`, `Entram: Cable Curls`, `Saem: Standing Barbell Curls`, and the start leads with `Começar Lower A`. `e2e/blocks.mjs` drives this, and the version 2 migration.
- **Runs.** On a new 3-day plan, `putStore` version 3 sessions on each of the last 8 Mondays with a done set of `Flat Barbell Bench Press`, `Dumbbell Lateral Raises` and `Standing Barbell Curls`, and the plans with those rows' `since` 9 Mondays ago (a new plan's runs start today, so without it nothing is due), then reload: `Exercícios a rever` lists the lateral raises and the curls (8 weeks done), not the bench press (no limit, `semana 9 · sem limite`). `e2e/runs.mjs` drives this, both buttons included.
- **Watch.** Import an export.zip as in [backup](./backup.md) `backup-watch-workouts`, then open `#treino/<its date>`: `page.getByRole('list', { name: 'Treinos do relógio' }).locator('li')` texts are the lines, and `#comecar-treino` reads `Registar as séries`. `e2e/watch-workouts.mjs` drives this.
- **Phone.** `{ mobile: true }`: no horizontal scroll on either tab (`e2e/workout.mjs` checks `Plano de treino` at 375 px too, where each `.muscle-days` wraps under its bar); each ✓ is at least 44 × 44.

## Gotchas

- Exercises are matched by name ignoring case and surrounding spaces, so `Supino` and `supino ` are one Exercise. Catalog names are English; the rest of the UI is Portuguese.
- `.role-pick`'s text includes the hidden select's options; read `.role-label`.
- A session's title follows the Workout Day's current name; a session whose day was deleted, or whose plan was dropped by the migration, reads `Dia de treino eliminado`, and keeps its sets.
- Plans before version 3 (version 1 had days on the plan, version 2 blocks of any days) load as their split when every block has 3 or 4 days, keeping names, ids, sets, reps, RIR and start, with the first Exercise of each day as `Principal` and the rest `Isolamento`. Any other plan is dropped; the old text is kept under `workout-tracking:workout-plans:migrated:<time>`, and the active plan moves to the first kept one.
- Sessions before version 3 load with each set with reps done and each empty one pending, no RIR and no target.
- The block and week are worked out from the sessions each time; nothing about them is stored but the start. The same holds for Exercise Runs: a Planned Exercise stores only `weeks` and `since`; one stored without them reads as its role's weeks and `since: null`.
- A pending set does not count as logged: it is skipped by the suggestion, the session counts and the training weeks.
- An After-effect must be dated after its session and name the session as a whole or one of its Exercises; removing the Exercise from the session removes its After-effects.
- Never drive port 5180.
