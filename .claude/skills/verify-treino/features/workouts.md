# Workouts (Treino)

The `Treino` view is the user's workout log. It opens on three tabs in a `nav` named `Treino`, with the theme toggle at the end of that row: `Registo` logs the sessions of one date, and `Plano de treino` holds the user's Workout Plans, and `Material` holds the Locais and each Exercise's material (see [equipment](./equipment.md)). A plan is a Training Split, 3 days (`Push`, `Pull`, `Full body`) or 4 (`Upper A`, `Lower A`, `Upper B`, `Lower B`), made of Training Blocks that each have those days. A new plan comes filled from the split's template with English catalog exercises (ported from just-workout); the user swaps, adds and removes them. Starting a session fills each set with a suggested target (double progression from last time), and one tap on ✓ marks a set done as shown.

## Sub-features

- `workout-open` opens the view on a bare URL or `#treino`, on today. `#treino/YYYY-MM-DD` opens that date.
- `workout-plan-create` is the card `Novo plano de treino`: shown when there is no plan, or after `+ Novo plano` in the `nav` named `Planos de treino` (with `Cancelar`). It has the textbox `Nome` (default `Plano de treino <n>`), the group `Divisão` with two `.split-tile` buttons (`3 dias` `Push · Pull · Full body`, `4 dias` `Upper A · Lower A · Upper B · Lower B`; the chosen one `aria-pressed="true"`, 3 days by default) and `Criar plano`. The new plan has one `Bloco A` of 6 weeks, not light, and every day filled. The selected plan tab is the active plan; `Eliminar` deletes it after a confirm, and logged sessions stay.
- `workout-plan-rename` renames a plan (`e2e/plan-rename.mjs`) with the pencil `Mudar o nome do plano` beside its title: it opens the textbox `Nome do plano de treino` with `Guardar` (or Enter) and `Cancelar` (or Escape). The plan tab takes the new name; an empty name keeps the old one. Days cannot be renamed, added or removed. A muted line under the title names the split's days.
- `workout-blocks`, `workout-block-edit`, `workout-block-notice` and `workout-runs` are in [Training Blocks](./blocks.md).
- `workout-day` is the `nav` named `Dias de <block>`: one `.tab` per day of the split, the shown one `aria-current="page"`. Under it the day's card is a region named after the day.
- `workout-planned` is each row of the day: `.role-label` (`Principal`, `Secundário`, `Isolamento` or `Abs`, over the select `Função de <exercise>`), `.planned-name`, `Séries de <exercise>` (a whole number) × `Repetições de <exercise>` (`8`, `8-12`, `8–12` or `8 a 12`, shown `8–12` once it loses focus), `Semanas de <exercise>` (the Exercise Run, 1 to 52; empty is no limit, placeholder `∞`) and the unit `semanas`, `Trocar <exercise>` (⇄) and `Remover <exercise> de <day>`. A role sets the rep range and the run of a template or added row: Principal 6–8 with no limit, Secundário 8–10, Isolamento and Abs 10–15, each 8 weeks, 3 sets; changing the role later keeps the weeks. On the current block (the first before the cycle starts) each row's `.planned-main` ends with `.run-line` `semana <w> de <n>` (`semana <w> · sem limite`), or `.missing-line` `Rever: <n> semanas feitas` once the run is done. In a block after the first, an Exercise the block before has in none of its days reads `<exercise> · novo` (`.planned-name.planned-new`).
- `workout-muscles` shows the muscle groups. Beside the day's planned list (the card is `.workout-day.day-trains`, the list in `.day-planned`), the aside `Músculos de <day>` (`.day-muscle-sets`, heading `.day-muscles-title` `Músculos neste dia`) has one `.day-muscle` per trained group in the picker's filter order: `.muscle-name`, `.muscle-sets` `<n> séries` and a `.track.muscle-track` bar as wide as its sets over the day's most (Push: Peito 3, Ombros 6, Tríceps 3, Abdominais 3; Full body: Peito 3, Costas 6, Ombros 3, Quadríceps 3, Posteriores 3, Bíceps 3, Tríceps 3, Glúteos 3, Gémeos 3). The user's own Exercises are left out of it, and it is absent, the list full width, when no catalog Exercise is in the day. At ≤640px it sits above the list as a wrapped line with no bars. Below the day's card, the region `Músculos por semana` (`.block-muscles`, heading `.blocks-title`) puts a body heat map (`.volume-body`: two `.volume-figure`s, `Frente` and `Costas`) beside a ranked list. Each figure region of a muscle group (`.volume-region`, `data-group=<muscle>`, both halves drawn) is as dark as its sets over the block's most (`--volume`, 22% to 100%); an untrained group is `data-untrained`, a dashed amber outline. The list `Grupos musculares, do que tem mais séries ao que tem menos` has one `.volume-row` button per trained group, most sets first and equal ones in the picker's order: `.muscle-name`, a `.volume-track` bar with a dashed 10–20 band, `.volume-sets` `<n> séries` (`1 série`) and a `.volume-zone` tag (`abaixo` under 10, `na zona` 10–20, `acima` over 20); every row tied for the most is `data-top`. Untrained groups follow in the dashed list `Grupos sem séries`, `sem séries` with no tag. A tap on a row or a body region picks its group in both (`aria-pressed`, `data-selected`; the rest dim), and the row opens `.volume-detail` `<n>% das séries da semana · <days>` (`Nenhum exercício do plano trabalha este grupo.` when untrained); the same tap, or the card's own padding, clears it. `.volume-notes` (`Onde podes olhar`) lists, for information only: `Sem séries no plano: <groups>.`, `Abaixo de 10 séries por semana: <group> (<n>), ….`, `Acima de 20 séries por semana: ….` and `<group> tem <r>× as séries de <group>.` for chest/back, quads/hamstrings or biceps/triceps 1.5 times apart (`Nada a assinalar.` when none), then `Para referência: 10–20 séries por semana é um intervalo comum para crescer. Não é uma regra.` A set counts in full for each primary muscle of its catalog Exercise, each day once a week. The user's own Exercises read `Sem grupo muscular: Supino (4 séries)`; a muted line closes with `Séries planeadas por semana, contando cada dia uma vez.` It follows `Séries de <exercise>`, swaps, adds and removals at once. The 3-day template gives Costas 12, Ombros 12, Peito 6, Bíceps 6, Tríceps 6, Abdominais 6, Quadríceps 3, Posteriores 3, Glúteos 3, Gémeos 3, and Antebraços untrained. `e2e/muscle-volume.mjs` drives all of this.
- `workout-picker` and `workout-muscle-map` are the exercise picker (`Adicionar exercício`, `Trocar exercício`), each catalog row with a `Ver músculos de <exercise>` figure button that shows where the Exercise is felt (`Alvo`, `Apoio`) on a body map: see [picker](./picker.md).
- `workout-date` is the `.date-stepper` region named `Data do treino`, which moves the logged date with `Dia anterior` and `Dia seguinte`. `Dia seguinte` is disabled on today, and `Voltar a hoje` shows on any other date. The date is in the URL hash.
- `workout-start` offers, on a date with no session, `Começar <day>` (primary): the day after the latest session's day on or before the date, counted by its place in the block, so the rotation carries into a new block; the first day when no session started from this plan. Then the other days of the current block (its first block before the cycle starts) and `Treino livre`. Above them, `.block-line` reads `<block> · semana <w> de <n>`, or just `<block>` before the cycle starts, plus ` · leve` for a light block.
- `workout-suggest` gives one group per planned Exercise with the planned number of sets, each pending at the suggested target: from the done sets of the latest earlier session with any, at the last set's load, each read against the RIR Target it aimed at (the kept target's `rir`–`rirMax`, light when it has `light: true`, or the Role's normal one when none was kept: Principal, Secundário `2`; Isolamento, Abs `1–3`; in a light block the session aims 2 higher, `4` and `3–5`): inside it or no RIR +1 rep, above it +2, below it but not 0 the same, RIR 0 −1. The hardest set decides for all; a change of target shifts the reps by the difference of their lower ends, and reps never rise more than 2 over the lowest done reps. Above the range the load goes up one step (1 kg under 25 kg, 2,5 kg from 25 kg), below it down one, reps re-estimated with Epley; a bodyweight Exercise (no kg) moves reps only. `.lift-note` reads `Plano <sets> × <range>. ` and the reason, e.g. `Última: 60 kg × 8 · RIR 1, alvo 2. Mais duro do que o alvo: mantém 60 kg × 8.`, `… Chegou à falha: faz 7 com 60 kg.`, `… Sobraram repetições: faz 13 com 10 kg.`, the RIR being the deciding set's (the one giving the fewest reps), or with none logged on it only the target, `Última: 62,5 kg × 7 · alvo 2. Faz 8 com 62,5 kg.` A change of target adds, after the first sentence, `Semana leve: alvo RIR 4.` into a light block, `Volta ao alvo RIR 2.` out of one (only when the kept target has `light: true`), or `Novo alvo: RIR 2.` for any other change (a new Role, or a session from before RIR Targets that kept its single old RIR, even `4`). An Exercise never done starts with empty sets and no reason.
- `workout-sets` is each `.set-row`: `Kg da série <n> de <exercise>` (comma decimals; empty is bodyweight, placeholder `—`) × `Repetições da série <n> de <exercise>`, `.set-rir` `RIR <n>` when set, the ✓ button `Série <n> de <exercise> feita` (`aria-pressed`; disabled with no reps; again un-marks) and `Remover série <n> de <exercise>`. Typing never marks a set done. The first Exercise with a set to do shows the group `RIR da série <n> de <exercise>` of `RIR <0–4 | 5 ou mais> na série <n> de <exercise>` chips below its sets, named for its first pending set; on an Exercise started with a target, every RIR chip inside the kept target's range has `.rir-target` (`2`, or `1 2 3`), and a pick also fills the next pending set with no RIR. `Adicionar série a <exercise>` copies the last set, pending; `Remover <exercise>` removes the Exercise from the session. `.lift-count` reads `<done>/<sets>`.
- `workout-collapse` turns an Exercise whose sets are all done into one `.lift-summary` button (`<exercise>` and `.lift-check` `✓ <n> séries`); tapping reopens it, with `Fechar`. The session's foot reads `.sets-done` `<done>/<total> séries feitas`, then `.session-muscles` `Músculos: <group> <sets> · …`: the muscle groups the done sets trained, in the picker's order, each set counted for every primary muscle of its catalog Exercise (`Músculos: Ombros 3`). It follows each ✓ and is absent while no catalog Exercise has a done set.
- `workout-session-exercise` adds an Exercise to a session through `Exercício` and the picker. It starts with the sets it had the last time it was done, pending, or one empty set.
- `workout-exercise-note` is the `Nota` toggle beside `+ Série` in each Exercise's group (named `Nota de <exercise>`, `aria-expanded`). It opens a textarea of the same name, placeholder `Dores, desconforto, como correu`, with a `Ditar nota` button and `.note-times` once it holds text. Closed with text, the toggle shows `.notes-dot`. It opens by itself on load when the note has text.
- `workout-notes` uses `Notas do treino`, about the workout as a whole, with `.note-times` under it (`Escrito a 6 out, 18:50 · editado 19:02`).
- `workout-end-feel` is the group `No fim do treino` in a session's foot, before `Notas do treino`: `No fim do treino: <word>` buttons on the five words from `Muito mal` to `Muito bem`. The pressed one has `aria-pressed="true"`; tapping it again removes it. Stored as the session's `feel`, 1 to 5.
- `workout-after-list` is the group `Depois do treino` under it, present once the session has After-effects. Each `.after-item` reads when (`Dia seguinte`, `2 dias depois`, `3 dias depois`, or the date further on), what (`Treino` or the Exercise's name), the note text and its `.note-times`, by date, the whole session first.
- `workout-recent` is the region `Treinos anteriores`, above the date's session cards, on today or a past date. It lists each session one to three days before the date, newest first, as a group named `<title>, <ontem | há 2 dias | há 3 dias>`, with `.recent-meta` `ontem · no fim: Bem` when the session has an end feel. In each group the textbox `Como está o corpo` is the After-effect of the whole session for the date, and each Exercise has a `Nota de <exercise>` toggle (`aria-expanded`, `.notes-dot` when closed with text, open by itself when it has a note for the date) that opens a textbox of the same name. Blank text removes the After-effect. After-effects of the session from other dates show under `Noutros dias`, read only. The card is absent when no session falls in those three days, and on a future date.
- `workout-delete` uses `Eliminar treino`, after a confirm.
- `workout-another` uses `+ Outro treino neste dia` to start a second session on a date that has one.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Opening the app, on a bare URL or `#treino`.

## Driving it with session.mjs

Preconditions:

- Fresh context, which opens on `Treino`. There is no workout plan and no session. Build state through the UI. `e2e/_driver.mjs` has `createWorkoutPlan(page, { name, days })`, `workoutDay(page, name)`, `addExercise(scope, name)`, `swapExercise(scope, from, to)` and `pickExercise(page, name)`.

- **Plan.** Click `Plano de treino` in the `nav` named `Treino`, then `createWorkoutPlan(page, { name: 'Força', days: 3 })`. The `Dias de Bloco A` tabs are `Push`, `Pull`, `Full body`; Push's `.planned-name`s are `Flat Barbell Bench Press`, `Standing Overhead Barbell Press`, `Dumbbell Lateral Raises`, `Cable Press-Downs`, `Cable Crunches`, `.role-label`s `Principal`, `Secundário`, `Isolamento`, `Isolamento`, `Abs`. Click `Trocar Flat Barbell Bench Press`: the dialog's region `Mesmo movimento` lists `Flat Dumbbell Bench Press` first. `addExercise(push, 'Supino')` creates it as `Isolamento`; searching `supino ` again shows it disabled under `Os teus`.
- **First session.** Click `Registo`, then `Dia anterior`, then `Começar Push`. A never-done Exercise has empty sets and its ✓ is disabled. Fill kg and reps, then ✓ each set: the Exercise collapses to `✓ <n> séries`, and `.sets-done` counts.
- **Persist.** `page.reload()`: values, ticks and notes are back. `app.stored()['workout-sessions']` is version 4; each set is `{ kg, reps, rir, done }`, an Exercise started with history has `target: { kg, reps, rir }`. `app.stored()['workout-plans']` is version 4 with `split` `ppf` or `ul`.
- **Suggestion.** These numbers need the setup of `e2e/workout.mjs`: Supino set to `Principal`, `4` sets, `6-8` (the bodyweight example is the `Secundário` 8–10 `Standing Overhead Barbell Press`), with no `Começar aqui`. On the `Isolamento` 10–15 row `addExercise` makes, the same sets suggest 10 × `60` (`…por isso desce para 60 kg e faz 10.`). Another day, `Push`: Supino after 8 × 60 and 7 × `62,5` (no RIR, no target) suggests 8 × `62,5` on every set, pending, `.lift-note` ending `Última: 62,5 kg × 7 · alvo 2. Faz 8 com 62,5 kg.`; a bodyweight 10, 9, 8 at RIR 2, 2 and none suggests 9 reps with no kg (`Última: peso corporal × 10/9/8 · alvo 2. Faz 9 com peso corporal.`, the deciding set having no RIR). `Começar` names the day after the last one: `Começar Pull`.
- **Phone.** `{ mobile: true }`: no horizontal scroll on either tab (`e2e/workout.mjs` and `e2e/muscle-volume.mjs` check `Plano de treino` at 375 px too, where the body sits above the list and each `.volume-track` wraps under its row); each ✓ is at least 44 × 44.

## Gotchas

- Exercises are matched by name ignoring case and surrounding spaces, so `Supino` and `supino ` are one Exercise. Catalog names are English; the rest of the UI is Portuguese.
- `.role-pick`'s text includes the hidden select's options; read `.role-label`.
- A session's title follows the Workout Day's current name; a session whose day was deleted, or whose plan was dropped by the migration, reads `Dia de treino eliminado`, and keeps its sets.
- Sessions before version 3 load with each set with reps done and each empty one pending, no RIR and no target. A kept target is `{ kg, reps, rir }` plus `rirMax` (above `rir`) for a range, without it the single RIR `rir`, and `light: true` when a light block's, without it not light. Sessions are stored at version 4; version 3 loads with all these keys, the old text kept under `workout-tracking:workout-sessions:migrated:<time>`. A document of a version this build doesn't know is kept under `:unreadable:` and left in place: the app shows no sessions and doesn't write over it until one is logged.
- A pending set does not count as logged: it is skipped by the suggestion, the session counts and the training weeks.
- An After-effect must be dated after its session and name the session as a whole or one of its Exercises; removing the Exercise from the session removes its After-effects.
- Never drive port 5180.
