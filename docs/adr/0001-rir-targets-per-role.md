# RIR Targets per Role, at about 80% effort

Each Role aims at its own RIR Target instead of a per-week RIR shared by every Exercise: 2 for Principal and Secundário, 1–3 for Isolamento and Abs. That is about 80% of the reps a set could do. Jeff Nippard and the studies he cites end most sets at 1–2 RIR, and nothing backs aiming at failure (`docs/research/rir-and-volume.md`). The target is a recommendation the Suggested Target reads, never a limit on what can be logged.

## Considered Options

- **A per-week RIR ramp in each Training Block** (the old 4–5 down to 0–1). Rejected: it pushed every block towards failure and gave a heavy compound and a high-rep isolation lift the same target.
